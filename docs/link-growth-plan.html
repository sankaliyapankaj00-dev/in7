CodeAiMan & gaming network  ·  from in7co.in  ·  26 July 2026

The one idea this plan is built on

Google counts referring domains, not links. 900 links from one website are worth roughly what 5 links from that website are worth. Everything below follows from that single fact. The client has asked that all work stay inside this one project, which caps what is achievable; section 8 states that plainly rather than overselling it.

2. The rules for everything that follows

RuleDetail
Domains over linksOne link from a new domain beats fifty more from an existing one. Every month should add referring domains, not link count.
No site-wide linksNever put an outbound link in a footer, sidebar or header again. It is the fastest way to rebuild the footprint that was just removed.
Cap per pageMaximum 3 outbound links to any one destination per page. Keep the site average under 10 outbound links per page.
Vary the anchorRotate through brand name, bare domain, and descriptive phrases. content/codaiman.ts already holds anchor pools and a link(key, seed) helper. Use it.
Topical fit firstIf a link would look odd to a human reader on that page, it will look odd to a crawler. Do not force it.
Stagger everythingNever publish a large batch at once again. See the schedule below.
Nothing paid, nothing exchangedNo paid placements, no reciprocal link deals, no directory blasts. Those are the categories that attract manual review.
The thing that was done wrong the first time

87 new pages went live on a site that had 11, all in one day, carrying 2,169 outbound links. That is a large content and link velocity spike on an established domain. It cannot be undone, but it should not be repeated. From here, everything ships in small batches.

3. The growth schedule

Constraint set by the client, 26 July

All work stays inside this project. No links are to be added from the client sites on the working machine, including sai computer bazar and yogi engineering works. That rules out adding referring domains, so the plan below works within what one domain can do.

Be clear about what that constraint means, because it changes the ceiling. in7co.in is one referring domain. It has already given codaiman.com what a single domain can give. Adding a hundred more links from it does not add a hundred links of value; it adds close to nothing, plus footprint. So the schedule below is not a link-building schedule. It is a content schedule that happens to carry a small number of links, which is the only version of this that is both safe and worth doing.

Month 1 — publish nothing, watch Submit the updated sitemap in Search Console. Watch indexing and impressions across the 98 existing pages. The July batch was large; if it caused a problem this is when it surfaces, and stacking more content on top would hide the signal. Doing nothing for four weeks is the correct action here, not a delay.
Month 2 onward — small content batches, roughly fortnightly Four to six new pages per batch, never more. Each page carries at most two or three outbound links, placed where a reader would actually follow them. Suggested order, aimed at the products with the thinnest coverage today:
  • CWS (8 links now): coding interview preparation, Python or JavaScript first, portfolio projects that get you hired, common beginner mistakes.
  • Fluenzo (8 links now): English vocabulary building, interview English, pronunciation for Indian speakers, English for children at home.
  • siteIQ (15 links now): schema markup guide, redirect chains, crawl budget, mobile usability.
Ongoing — hold the caps Site average stays under 10 outbound links per page. No single page points at one destination more than three times. No new site-wide links anywhere, ever. Anchors rotate through the pools in content/codaiman.ts.
Honest ceiling

Following this for six months adds maybe 40 to 60 more contextual links from the same single domain. In search terms that is a small effect, and most of it is the value of the content itself rather than the links. The plan is safe and it is worth doing for the traffic the content earns. It will not move codaiman.com's authority much, because authority comes from referring domains and this plan cannot add any.

4. The highest-value action: move the content, not the links

There are 50 pages of genuinely good content on in7co.in right now: 32 tool guides and 18 technical articles. They are on the wrong domain. A gaming site publishing PDF guides and Core Web Vitals articles is a topical mismatch, and Google's site reputation abuse policy is aimed at exactly that pattern.

On codaiman.com the same content is perfectly natural, and stops being a link-building exercise entirely:

How to do the migration

  1. Clone the codaiman.com repository onto the working machine. This is currently the blocker; nothing else is.
  2. Port content/tools-*.ts and content/tech-*.ts across. They are plain TypeScript data files with no dependency on this site, so the move is mechanical.
  3. Convert the outbound codaiman.com links into internal links.
  4. Publish in batches of roughly 10 pages a fortnight, not all 50 at once.
  5. Once live, 301 the in7co.in versions to the codaiman.com equivalents. That passes whatever authority the pages accumulated and removes the duplicate.
Client decision recorded 26 July

Content on in7co.in stays in place for now. Nothing was deleted; only link density was reduced. The migration above is agreed in principle and is the intended direction, pending repository access.

The realistic summary

With the current constraint, section 4 is the plan. Moving the 50 tool and tech pages to codaiman.com is worth more than any further link work from in7co.in, because those pages would rank on a domain that fits their subject and would feed authority into the four product pages internally. It is also the only remaining action with no downside risk.

6. What to watch, and what would mean stop

Check monthly in Search Console

  • Indexed page count on in7co.in. A sudden drop after the July additions would be the first sign of a problem.
  • Impressions and clicks on the original 11 gaming pages specifically. If those fall while nothing else changed, the new sections are hurting the host site.
  • Manual actions report on both properties. This is the one that matters most and takes ten seconds to check.
  • Referring domain count for codaiman.com. This is the number the plan is trying to move, not total links.
Stop conditions

Any of the following means pause all link work and reassess rather than continuing the schedule: a manual action on either property, a sustained drop in indexed pages, or a fall in impressions on the original gaming pages that does not recover within a month. Adding more links into a declining signal makes diagnosis impossible.

7. Things explicitly not to do

  • Do not add more links from in7co.in. That domain has given what it can give. More volume from it adds risk and no value.
  • Do not rebuild footer link columns anywhere.
  • Do not buy links, submit to link directories, or enter reciprocal link arrangements.
  • Do not use the same anchor text at scale. It is the single clearest manufactured-link signal.
  • Do not publish another large batch of pages in one day on any property.
  • Do not point every new link at the homepage. Spread them across service and product pages.

8. Realistic expectation

With all work confined to in7co.in, the honest forecast is modest. Six months of fortnightly content batches adds perhaps 40 to 60 contextual links from a single referring domain, plus whatever traffic the new pages earn on their own. The second part is the real return; the first is close to a rounding error in search terms.

The number that would actually move is referring domains, and this plan cannot move it. That is a consequence of the constraint, not a failure of the plan, and it is written down here so the expectation is set correctly rather than discovered in six months.

If the goal is genuinely to raise codaiman.com's search position, section 4 is the action: move the content to the domain it belongs on. Everything else on this page is maintenance.
